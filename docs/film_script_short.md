# Crosscheck: the film, short cut (under 3 minutes)

Narration for `uv run python cli.py film --cut short`. The same scenes and numbers as
`film_script.md`, said more briefly, without the flaps scene. Sections missing here are not filmed.

## 01 · Title
Crosscheck. A monitoring pilot that only sees and hears.

## 02 · Why
In thirty-one of thirty-seven airline accidents involving the crew, the NTSB found that someone
didn't monitor, or didn't challenge. Crosscheck always does.

## 03 · How it works
It listens to the radio and intercom with Muse Voice Transcribe, reads the displays through a
camera with Muse Spark, and checks what was said, what was set, and what the aircraft is doing.

## 04 · Altitude bust
Air traffic control clears Alaska four two one to eleven thousand feet. The crew reads it back,
then sets twelve thousand. Crosscheck speaks up with seventeen seconds to spare.

## 05 · Autothrottle
On final, the autothrottle drops out and the speed decays: the Asiana two one four pattern.
Crosscheck calls it before the speed is ten knots low.

## 07 · Pilot watch
An optional camera watches the captain, and SAM 3D Body measures his posture. He slumps;
Crosscheck asks, gets no answer, and tells the first officer to take control. Muse Wonder,
watching on its own, agrees.

## 08 · Evidence
Every run is scored against the simulator. Fifteen minutes of normal flying: no false alarm.

## 09 · Models
Seven Meta models, each doing one job: hearing, reading, watching, checking, and a second opinion.

## 10 · Close
One more pair of eyes and ears on the flight deck. Crosscheck.
